# Dr Nazia Gillani

**Electronics & Embedded Systems Engineer | Concept-to-Product Development | Prototyping, Verification & Validation | Sensor Systems & Signal Processing | Edge & Explainable AI**

I develop electronic and embedded products from user and client needs through requirements, system architecture, circuit and PCB design, firmware, hardware–software integration, prototype bring-up, verification and product handover. My experience spans commercial electronics, healthcare technology and multi-sensor systems.

I combine hands-on electronics and embedded development with signal processing and end-to-end data and model-development pipelines. From sensor acquisition, calibration, noise filtering, and feature engineering through model development, validation and deployment-ready implementation. I am committed to developing explainable AI systems and translating the signal features that influence model outputs into clear, application-specific insights. This enables engineering teams, clients and domain experts to evaluate system performance, understand limitations and make informed product decisions.

I work confidently in client-facing, multidisciplinary teams, translating technical and user needs into practical system requirements, communicating hardware, software and model trade-offs clearly, and progressing integrated prototypes through structured verification and validation.

## Selected product and engineering work

### High-speed FPGA electronics platform — commercial hardware development

- Produced 28+ page schematics and implementation constraints for complex 6- and 8-layer FPGA boards, covering digital, mixed-signal, power and reset circuitry.
- Engineered high-speed interfaces including PCIe, Ethernet, USB, SATA and LVDS, with controlled-impedance, differential-pair and length-matching requirements.
- Supported direct design reviews with US client engineers and delivered component libraries, BOMs, netlists, design documentation and manufacturing-handover outputs.
- Built and maintained a 1,000+ component ECAD library and introduced practical SOPs and design checklists to improve consistency and reuse.

### Lower-limb robotics and wearable sensing — embedded product development

- Developed electronics and embedded C/C++ firmware for lower-limb exoskeleton prototypes using STM32/ESP32 and FPGA-based platforms, motor drivers, sensors and wireless data logging.
- Implemented real-time acquisition, state-machine control, filtering, sensor fusion, system debugging and validation across hardware and software.
- Co-developed four battery-powered 9-axis IMU nodes with Wi-Fi telemetry and Python-based acquisition for wearable gait analysis.
- Validated gait-processing algorithms over approximately 3,400 cycles with a median timing error below 25 ms, and trained nine engineers to operate and extend the platform.

### Thermal occupancy sensing — rapid hardware–software prototyping

- Built and integrated microcontroller, Raspberry Pi and thermal-sensor prototypes for privacy-aware occupancy sensing.
- Developed Python control, data-logging and automated-test workflows, then diagnosed integration issues across sensors, embedded hardware and software.
- Translated experimental requirements into working prototypes and repeatable data-collection procedures.

### MovementGuard — user-centred sensing product

Lead Systems Engineer for [MovementGuard](https://www.movementguard.com/), a privacy-preserving movement-sensing product for health and care applications.

- Own system-level development across sensing hardware, embedded acquisition, data processing, analytics and alert workflows.
- Translate input from clinicians, GPs, occupational therapists, patients and carers into product requirements and prototype priorities.
- Coordinate architecture decisions, prototype integration, technical risk resolution, testing and stakeholder evaluation across the product lifecycle.

### Real-time sensing, signal processing and Edge AI

- Developed end-to-end acquisition, signal-processing and machine-learning pipelines for movement sensing across radar, wearable and inertial data.
- Achieved tremor quantification with R² > 0.97 and fall detection with 98% accuracy, including a low-latency implementation with 0.08 ms runtime.
- Applied interpretable features and explainable analysis to support validation, engineering decisions and communication with healthcare stakeholders.
